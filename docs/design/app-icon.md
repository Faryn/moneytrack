# Spendie app icon

Approved on 2026-11-04: a sage wallet and ivory coin on a deep pine background. Related muted greens keep the palette cohesive, with the lighter wallet defining the silhouette and the warm coin providing contrast.

- Background target: pine `#183D36`.
- Wallet target: sage `#A6C6AF`.
- Clasp target: eucalyptus `#547E68`.
- Coin and fastener target: ivory `#F1E5C9`.

These are art-direction targets; shading in the generated raster varies around them.

The approved original is [moneytrack-icon-source.png](moneytrack-icon-source.png). The app uses [AppIcon.png](../../ios/MoneyTrack/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/AppIcon.png), resized proportionally to 1024×1024 for the existing universal iOS app-icon asset catalog. It is an opaque RGB PNG with square outer edges; iOS supplies the icon mask. The existing app accent and financial green/red semantics remain unchanged.

## Artwork provenance

Created with the built-in image-generation tool, then approved by the user. The original blue-background concept was edited with this final prompt:

> Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit the supplied MoneyTrack app-icon concept. Preserve the wallet silhouette, coin position, clasp, proportions, centered composition, square full-bleed canvas and generous margins. Change only the palette and finish to make the icon calmer and more cohesive. Replace the harsh electric blue background with a deep muted pine green, target #183D36. Recolor the wallet body to soft sage/seafoam green, target #A6C6AF. Use a middle eucalyptus green for the clasp, target #547E68, with a deeper forest recess. Recolor the coin and small clasp fastener to a warm ivory, target #F1E5C9. The related low-saturation greens form an analogous palette; the small warm ivory areas balance the cool greens. Maintain clear light-dark contrast between the sage wallet and dark pine background so the silhouette reads at small size. Use restrained matte shading and very subtle gradients, no neon, no bright blue, no vivid emerald glow, no shiny plastic. Do not change geometry or add text, letters, currency signs, borders, rounded outer masks, phones, labels, swatches or presentation elements. Output one clean opaque square app-icon proposal.
